Texture credits
===============

Source: CelestiaContent, https://github.com/CelestiaProject/CelestiaContent (textures/hires, 4096x2048).
Full per-map credits are in that repository's README and .license files. Maps are NASA/JPL/USGS-derived composites,
processed by the Celestia community. What was changed here: converted to JPEG, alpha channels dropped, and (for the nine
maps that already existed in this project) a per-channel mean/std colour match to the previous map so the swap on zoom does
not change the overall colour. Io, Europa, Ganymede and Callisto are unmodified apart from format/size.

solar/hi/earth_specular.jpg  is the alpha channel of CelestiaContent's earth.png (matched to hi/earth.jpg), mean-matched to the old mask.
solar/hi/earth_height.jpg    is elev_bump_4k.jpg from https://github.com/turban/webgl-earth (images/), linearly rescaled to the value range of
                       the previous textures/solar/earth_height.jpg. That repository states no licence or provenance in its README, so
                       check it before redistributing.

If you redistribute or sell this project, check these terms first; solar/hi/uranus.jpg is the restrictive one, solar/hi/earth_height.jpg is unverified.

sun, sun_flare (textures/solar/ and textures/solar/hi/) are procedurally generated (no third-party imagery, no licence restrictions).
